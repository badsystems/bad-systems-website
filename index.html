The Framework

The BAD Methodology

Three intentional pillars that transform how you build, run, and grow a service business — without sacrificing what matters most.

B
Build
Intentional Foundations

Before you automate or delegate, you need clarity. Build means designing the core of your business with purpose — your offers, processes, and vision — so everything that follows is aligned.

Core offer architecture Process documentation Strategic clarity mapping Brand & positioning
A
Automate
Aligned Technology

Technology should serve your life, not consume it. Automate means implementing smart, human-first tools that handle the repetitive — freeing your energy for the creative and the strategic.

Workflow automation CRM & client systems Communication flows Reporting & analytics
D
Delegate
Sustainable Expansion

Growth requires letting go. Delegate means building the team structures, SOPs, and leadership systems that let you expand without bottlenecking everything through yourself.

Team structure design SOP creation Leadership frameworks Hiring & onboarding
